2. About Rify

Rify is a location-based platform that allows users to request, share, and access real-time, community-generated information about nearby places.

Content on the App includes:

  • User Requests: Questions posted by users about nearby locations (e.g. "Is this café open?"), answered by other users with camera-captured photos.
  • Public Service Announcements (PSAs): Short text descriptions with camera-captured photos, posted to alert other users to nearby conditions or events.
  • Daily Tasks: Location-based tasks at nearby points of interest that users can resolve by submitting a photo.
  • Community Requests: Algorithmically generated, location-relevant questions seeded by Rify at nearby points of interest. These are clearly labelled "Community Request" in the App and are distinguishable from questions posted by individual users.

All user-generated content is provided for informational purposes only. Rify does not curate, verify, or endorse any content posted by users.

10. Push Notifications

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (a service provided by Google LLC) to send push notifications to your device. You will be asked for permission to receive notifications when you first launch the App.

Notification types include:

  • Alerts about new user requests or PSAs posted near your location;
  • Confirmation that someone has answered your user request;
  • Notifications that someone has appreciated a photo you submitted.

Notifications about nearby activity are based on your approximate location using a geohash grid. Your precise GPS coordinates are not transmitted to other users at any time.

You can withdraw your consent to receive push notifications at any time in two ways:

  • In-app: Go to Settings → App Settings within Rify and toggle off "Push Notifications". This immediately unsubscribes your device from all nearby-activity topics and removes your token from our servers.

Withdrawing consent will not affect your ability to use the App, but you will no longer receive real-time alerts about nearby activity.
